# About Ashton's Auto Detail

Ashton's Auto Detail is a mobile car detailing service based in Duncan, BC, founded and operated by Ashton, a young local entrepreneur from the Cowichan Valley.

## The Story

The business was started to solve a simple problem: people in Duncan and the Cowichan Valley needed professional, affordable detailing without the hassle of dropping their car off at a shop. The solution is a fully mobile service that comes directly to the customer's home or workplace.

Every job uses P&S professional-grade detailing chemicals, the same brand trusted by high-volume detail shops, rental fleets, and car enthusiasts across North America.
